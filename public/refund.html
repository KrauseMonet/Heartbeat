Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

7-day money-back guarantee

All premium subscriptions are eligible for a full refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions asked.

  • Applies to both monthly and yearly plans
  • Full refund to your original payment method
  • Processed within 5–7 business days

How to request a refund

  1. 1
    Email us at hello@heartbeat.app within 7 days of your purchase date.
  2. 2
    Include the email address you used to sign up, or your room code — whichever is easier for you to find.
  3. 3
    We'll confirm receipt and process your refund. It will appear on your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

After the 7-day window

Monthly plans: No refund is available for the current billing period once 7 days have passed since purchase.

Yearly plans: No partial refunds are issued for unused months after the 7-day window has closed. However, you can cancel at any time to stop future renewals.

Exceptions

Accounts found to have violated our Terms of Service — including misuse, fraudulent activity, or sharing credentials in violation of our terms — are not eligible for refunds.

Refund eligibility is per subscription period. If you have previously received a refund for the same account, a second refund may not be granted at our discretion.

Cancellation vs. refund

Cancelling your subscription stops future billing but does not automatically trigger a refund for your current period. If you would like a refund within the 7-day window, please email us — cancellation alone is not sufficient to initiate a refund.
