Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Who we are

Heartbeat is a private space for couples in long-distance relationships. It is built and operated by Koustav Mishra, based in Bangalore, India.

If you have any questions about this policy, email us at mishrakoustav01@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to make the app work for you and your partner.

  • Account information — your email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Profile information — your photo, timezone, birthday, and status message if you choose to add them. All optional.
  • Content you create — love notes, memories, bucket list items, gratitude messages, and game answers. This content is shared only with your partner.
  • Usage data — heartbeat taps, mood updates, streak counts, and notification tokens. Used only to power the app's features.
  • Device token — if you allow notifications, we store a device token to send you push notifications from your partner.
We do not collect your location. We do not track your behaviour across other apps or websites. We do not build advertising profiles.

How we use your data

  • To sync your content with your partner in real time.
  • To send you notifications from your partner when you allow them.
  • To remember your preferences like timezone and display name.
  • To maintain your streak and together days counter.

We do not use your data for advertising. We do not sell your data to any third party. We never will.

Who can see your data

Your content — notes, memories, game answers, gratitude messages — is visible only to you and your partner. Nobody else can access your room.

We use Firebase (by Google) to store data and handle authentication. Firebase stores data on Google's servers. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

We use Cloudinary to store photos you upload. You can read Cloudinary's privacy policy at cloudinary.com/privacy.

Heartbeat's team does not read your personal messages, notes, or game responses. Your conversations are yours.

Sensitive content

Heartbeat includes optional adult content features for couples who choose to use them. This content is stored in your private room and is never visible to anyone other than you and your partner. It is never used for any purpose other than delivering it to your partner within the app.

Data retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all your personal data — including your profile, notes, memories, and game history — is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days.

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab inside the app.

Your rights

Wherever you are in the world, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email mishrakoustav01@gmail.com or use the delete account option in the app.

Children

Heartbeat is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy we will notify you inside the app before the changes take effect. The date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent update.
