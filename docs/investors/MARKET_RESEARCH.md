## Users and behaviour

- **Women's share:** women are about 26% of users on Indian dating platforms (Redseer, Dec 2025).
- **Paying users:** typically 5–15% of monthly active users (Redseer, Dec 2025).
- **Swiping fatigue:** more than 75% of Gen Z app users report it (Redseer, Dec 2025; the underlying survey isn't named). A US survey found 79% Gen Z burnout ([Forbes Health/OnePoll](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/new-forbes-study-explores-dating-app-burnout/)).
- **Intent apps vs swipe apps:** in Q3 2025, "serious" apps grew paying users 17% while a swipe app fell 7% (Redseer).
- **Serious relationships:** 58% of Indian dating-app users want one (YouGov India 2025, as cited by [Aisle/GDI](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/featured/aisle-implements-money-back-guarantee-as-it-marks-12-years/); original not checked).

## Competitors

- **Tinder:** most-used dating app among Indian men and women ([Rakuten Insight via Statista, Aug 2024](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1182923/india-leading-mobile-dating-application-by-gender/)).
- **Bumble:** 40% of female respondents use it (same survey).
- **Hinge:** India is its #2 market by downloads; Hinge+ costs ₹1,199/month and HingeX ₹1,499/month ([GDI, Sep 2026](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/from-the-web/hinge-india-second-largest-market/)). Paying users grew 17% in Q4 2025 while Match Group's total fell 5% ([Match Q4 2025](https://finviz.com/news/298114/match-group-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-results)).
- **Aisle:**
  - **Ownership:** Info Edge bought 76% for ₹91 Cr in 2022 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/entrepreneurship/news/story/infoedge-acquires-76-stake-in-dating-app-aisle-for-rs-91-cr-325207-2022-03-08)) and owned 100% by Nov 2025 ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/info-edges-jeevansathi-to-acquire-remaining-stake-in-dating-app-aisle-for-rs-5-5-crore-84235.htm)).
  - **Revenue:** ₹39.6 Cr in FY25.
  - **Pricing:** weekly plans from ₹99.
- **TrulyMadly:** revenue ₹12.3 Cr in FY25; about US$8.9M raised ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/trulymadly/)).
- **QuackQuack:** 40M+ users, led by tier-2 and tier-3 cities (company claim, 2025).
- **FRND:** audio-led, game-like dating; US$6.5M Series A led by KRAFTON in 2021 ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2021/12/bengaluru-dating-app-audio-romance-frnd-series-a-funding-krafton)).

## Precedents

- **OkCupid** (question-based matching): acquired by Match for US$50M in 2011.
- **Couples apps:**
  - **Paired:** US$4.6M raised.
  - **Lasting:** acquired by Talkspace for US$10.7M in 2020.
  - **Agapé:** about US$2.5M raised.
- **Thursday** (UK): closed its app around 2025 because of declining interest and moved to in-person events. A reminder that offline events matter.
